## Design Context

### Users
Three personas at different times:
- **Security engineer on-call** — stressed, triaging an incident, needs critical info without scanning. Every extra word is friction.
- **Platform / ML engineer** — configuring policies, checking agent behavior, calm but focused. Expects technical precision.
- **CTO / engineering lead** — reviewing compliance posture and reports, wants confidence signals not raw data dumps.

### Brand Personality
Technical. Transparent. Trustworthy.

Voice: direct, precise, never condescending. Shows its work. Explains what's happening and why, without lecturing. No marketing fluff anywhere in the UI. Treats users as competent engineers.

### Aesthetic Direction
Military/tactical dark. Dark backgrounds (`#0a0a08` to `#0f0f0d` warm near-black), amber (`#d97706`) as primary alert accent, green (`#22c55e`) for healthy/safe states, tight typography, dense data layouts, no decorative chrome. Feels like an ops dashboard — mission control for AI agents.

Anti-references: avoid cyan-on-dark AI aesthetic, avoid glassmorphism, avoid gradient text, avoid Inter as the only font.

### Design Principles
1. **Precision over personality** — every word earns its place. No filler, no passive voice, no "just" or "simply".
2. **State is always explained** — empty states, loading states, and errors always tell the user why and what to do next. A dead-end state is a UX failure.
3. **Severity is always legible** — critical information must be immediately distinguishable from normal. Use color, weight, and position — not just icons.
4. **Technical depth on demand** — surface the summary first, expose the detail on interaction. Treat the user as an expert.
5. **Confidence through clarity** — "All clear" means something when it's earned. State positive outcomes explicitly, not just the absence of negatives.
