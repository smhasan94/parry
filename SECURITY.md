# Security Policy

Parry is itself a security product, so we take vulnerabilities in
Parry seriously. This document explains how to report a security
issue, what to expect from us, and what we consider out of scope.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Email **security@parry.dev** with:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce (or a proof of concept)
- The affected component (`backend`, `sdk` (Python), `sdk-ts`,
  `sdk-go`, `dashboard`, `landing`, or `infra`)
- The version or git SHA you tested against
- Optional: your suggested fix

If your report involves an active exploitation of customer data on
the SaaS instance, also flag it as **urgent** in the subject line.

You can encrypt the report with the PGP key published at
`https://parry.dev/.well-known/pgp-key.txt`.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within 1 business day.
- **Initial triage** (severity assessment, scope confirmation) within
  3 business days.
- **Fix or mitigation timeline** communicated at triage. Critical
  issues affecting production data integrity ship as out-of-band
  patches; lower-severity issues bundle into the next release.
- **Public disclosure** coordinated with you. We default to
  disclosing in `CHANGELOG.md` after a fix is shipped and customers
  have had a reasonable upgrade window. We will not name you in the
  disclosure unless you ask us to.
- **No legal action** for good-faith research that follows this
  policy. We treat reporters as collaborators.

## Scope

In scope:

- The backend at `api.parry.dev`
- The dashboard at `app.parry.dev`
- The marketing site at `parry.dev`
- The published SDK packages: `parry` (PyPI), `@parry/sdk` (npm),
  `github.com/sharukhhasan/parry/sdk-go`
- The Docker images we publish for self-hosting

Out of scope (please don't report):

- Findings against forks, mirrors, or unofficial deployments.
- Theoretical issues with no demonstrable exploitation path.
- Missing security headers on `parry.dev` marketing pages where no
  customer data is handled.
- Rate limit bypasses on unauthenticated marketing endpoints.
- Self-XSS, social engineering of Parry employees, physical attacks.
- Reports generated by automated scanners with no manual validation.

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
| --- | --- |
| `main` (latest) | ✅ |
| Most recent tagged release | ✅ |
| Anything older | ❌ — please upgrade |

For self-hosted / on-prem customers, we backport critical fixes to
the most recent two minor versions.

## Hall of fame

Researchers who report valid issues that we ship a fix for will be
credited in the relevant `CHANGELOG.md` entry, with their consent.
